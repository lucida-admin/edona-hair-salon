Hi! I'm Edona.

I'm a second-generation stylist raised in my mom's hair salon in Austria. Watching her, I learned that truly great hair is more than just technique. It's about listening, connecting, and ensuring every client feels genuinely cared for.

I'm a Vidal Sassoon-trained stylist and a Redken Artist specializing in precision haircuts, dimensional color, and curly hair. Each appointment is a collaboration to create a look that feels effortless, elevated, and true to you.

Great hair has the power to reveal self-confidence, and helping clients feel that confidence is what I love most about my work.

When I'm not in the salon, you can find me outdoors, staying active, and spending time with friends.
